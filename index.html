01 / WHO I AM

I like working where data, technology and problem solving meet.

I work across data analytics, BI and data engineering, with hands on experience taking data from its raw form through to something useful, whether that’s a dashboard, an analytical insight, a data pipeline or a machine learning solution.

I’ve worked with SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Databricks, PySpark, AWS and ML/NLP technologies, building experience across different parts of the data lifecycle rather than specialising in just one layer.

From Lorgan’s AI powered university recommendation system to analytics and Power BI work at LTIMindtree, I’ve learned to look beyond the tool itself: understand the problem, work out what the data needs, build the solution, validate it and communicate what it means.

That’s the kind of data professional I’m building toward: someone who understands both the data and what needs to be done with it.
